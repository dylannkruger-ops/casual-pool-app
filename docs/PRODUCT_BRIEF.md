# Casual Pool — Product Brief

> Australia's casual workforce, on tap.

## 1. Vision

A two-sided staffing marketplace that lets Australian businesses fill a casual shift in minutes, and lets casual workers turn idle hours into income — with a transparent, flat **$4.99** platform fee on each side per hired shift. No markups on wages, no agency commissions, no subscription gatekeeping.

## 2. Problem

- Australian hospitality, retail, warehousing and events run on casual labour, but the booking flow is fractured across SMS, Facebook groups, and legacy agencies.
- Agencies take 25–40% markups and lock workers into exclusivity.
- Existing apps charge subscriptions or % cuts, hiding the real cost.
- ABN verification, licences (RSA, Forklift, White Card) and work rights are checked manually — slow and error-prone.

## 3. Solution

A premium mobile-first marketplace with:
- **Verified, two-sided profiles** — workers list rate, skills, licences, availability; businesses list ABN-verified company, industry, location.
- **One-tap apply / shortlist / hire** — minutes from post to confirmation.
- **Flat $4.99 fee** on hire (business) and on accept (worker).
- **Trust layer** — ABN verification, document checks, ratings, repeat-employer signals.
- **In-app messaging** unlocked only after shortlist or hire.

## 4. Pricing & Unit Economics

| Side | When charged | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Business | On hire | $4.99 |
| Worker | On accepting hire | $4.99 |
| **Total per filled shift** | | **$9.98** |

- Stripe fee at ~2.2% + $0.30 per charge → ~$0.41 cost on $9.98 of revenue ⇒ ~96% gross margin per filled shift.
- Target average filled shifts per active business / month: 8.
- Target average shifts accepted per active worker / month: 6.

## 5. Market

- ~2.6M casual workers in Australia (ABS, casual employment share of total employment).
- Hospitality + Retail + Warehousing + Events = the four wedge categories at launch.
- TAM (Australia, casual shifts/year × $4.99 × 2): meaningful nine-figure revenue ceiling without leaving AU.

## 6. Go-to-market

**Phase 1 — Surry Hills / Newtown / Alexandria liquidity wedge (NSW).**
- 200 hand-recruited verified workers across barista, bar, kitchen hand, event staff.
- 50 hospitality venues onboarded face-to-face.
- $0 fees for first 30 days for both sides to seed reviews + ratings.

**Phase 2 — Greater Sydney + Melbourne CBD.**
- Paid acquisition on Instagram / TikTok aimed at hospitality casuals and venue managers.
- Partnerships with RSA training providers + TAFE programs (worker pipeline).

**Phase 3 — National + new verticals (warehousing, retail, events).**

## 7. Defensibility

- **Verified supply** — verified workers + ABN-verified businesses compound trust.
- **Repeat-employer graph** — Casual Pool learns who hires who, so we can surface "your roster" suggestions agencies can't.
- **Pricing transparency** — flat fee is hard to undercut without losing margin.

## 8. Roadmap (12 months)

| Quarter | Theme | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | MVP launch | Auth, role onboarding, ABN gate, post/apply/hire, messaging, ratings |
| Q2 | Trust + scale | Document verification, ID checks, dispute flow, saved rosters |
| Q3 | Liquidity tools | Shift templates, recurring shifts, multi-location, team accounts |
| Q4 | Payments & payroll | Optional pass-through payroll, Stripe Connect, invoice export |

## 9. Risks & Mitigations

- **Liquidity in a new postcode.** → Hand-recruit supply first; offer fee-free month to anchor demand.
- **Worker no-shows.** → Reputation system + 24-hour cancellation rule + cancellation fees.
- **Off-platform leakage.** → Messaging unlocked only after hire; smart blocking of phone/email patterns in chat until hired.
- **Regulatory.** → Casual Pool is a marketplace, not an employer. Workers are direct hires by businesses. Standard Australian Fair Work / workplace laws apply.

## 10. KPIs

- Time-to-hire (median, minutes).
- Fill rate (% of posted shifts hired).
- Repeat-employer rate.
- 30-day worker activation (signup → first accepted shift).
- Net revenue per active business / month.
