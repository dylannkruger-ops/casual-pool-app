## [FAQ]

**What happens when an employee gets stuck?**
It stops. Every step is verified; after one careful retry it halts and asks you — with a screenshot of exactly where and why. Your employees never guess their way forward.

**Does my computer need to stay on?**
Yes — your team works your screen, so the machine stays awake and unlocked during a run. MOTE holds the screen and shows the face over your work. Walk away; your phone gets pinged if anyone needs a yes.

**Can they send emails or pay for things on their own?**
Sending anything needs your approval, every time — there's no setting to change that. Payments and money movement are out of scope entirely. You're the boss; some things stay on the boss's desk.

**Is this recording everything I do?**
No. MOTE captures only during runs you start or schedule, redacts by default, stores everything encrypted on your machine, and lets you delete any of it in one click. Our full threat model is public.

**What if I need an employee you don't have?**
Studio's builder: demonstrate the job once and MOTE drafts the new hire. Or request it — the roster grows monthly, and every employee joins only after clearing 95% on the bench.
