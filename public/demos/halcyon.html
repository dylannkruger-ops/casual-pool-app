Questions, answered

Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from your billing settings — changes are prorated automatically, and there's no penalty for switching either direction.
What happens after my free trial?
Every plan starts with a 14-day trial, no card required. If you don't upgrade, your workspace simply reverts to a read-only state — nothing is deleted and no charge ever happens without your confirmation.
Do you offer refunds?
If Halcyon isn't a fit, email us within 30 days of any charge and we'll refund it in full — no forms, no retention flow, no questions asked.
